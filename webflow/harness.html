What “working” looks like: the red rules mark the strict text-block edges. With the embed active, punctuation at line starts/ends (quotes, commas, periods, hyphens) should extend slightly beyond the red rules — the lines look optically aligned rather than ragged with punctuation. Dragging the window narrower/wider re-flows the text and the hanging punctuation recomputes on each resize. In the console you can call OpticalMargin.destroy(document.querySelector('[data-opticalmargin]')) to restore the plain markup, or OpticalMargin.refit() to re-apply.
